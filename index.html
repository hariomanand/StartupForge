₹10,000 Crore Fund

SIDBI-managed fund investing in SEBI-registered AIFs (VCs) that then invest in DPIIT-recognised startups. As of 2025, ₹21,276 Cr invested in 1,173+ startups. New ₹10,000 Cr announced in 2025 Budget.

Startup Launch Roadmap

Step-by-step guide from idea to fully-funded operational business — every action item covered

🎯 Validate Your Idea

Before spending ₹1, validate demand through:

  • Google Trends India — check 3-year search trend
  • Talk to 50 potential customers (don't skip this)
  • Build a landing page with Google Forms to capture interest
  • Check DPIIT startup database — is it already saturated?
  • Use AI (Claude/ChatGPT) to generate competitive analysis

📋 Legal Setup & Registration

  • Register as Pvt. Ltd. (recommended) via MCA21 portal — ₹7,000–₹15,000
  • Get PAN, GST registration (mandatory above ₹20L turnover)
  • Udyam Registration (MSME) — free, instant, unlocks 50+ benefits
  • DPIIT Startup Recognition — apply at startupindia.gov.in (free)
  • Open a startup current account (HDFC/Axis for best startup features)

💰 Secure Initial Funding

  • Apply SISFS via DPIIT incubators (₹20L grant, no equity dilution)
  • PM Mudra Yojana Shishu loan (₹50K, zero collateral)
  • AIM / Atal Incubation Centre for deep-tech
  • Apply to angel networks (IAN, LetsVenture) after MVP
  • State government startup fund (varies by state — see above)

🛠️ Build Your MVP

  • Tech startups: Use no-code tools (Bubble, Webflow) to launch in 2 weeks
  • Use Claude/GPT to generate code + documentation
  • Host on Vercel or Cloudflare Pages (free)
  • Use Supabase (free tier) for database + auth
  • Track metrics from Day 1 with Mixpanel (free) or Google Analytics

🚀 Launch & GTM Strategy

  • ProductHunt India launch for B2B SaaS startups
  • LinkedIn content + founder story — cheapest B2B channel
  • Partner with local incubators for their community
  • Register on GeM portal (Government e-Marketplace) for B2G sales
  • Attend CII, NASSCOM, TiE events for network + PR

📈 Scale & Series A Prep

  • Hit revenue milestones: ₹1 Cr ARR for pre-Series A
  • Build data room: financials, metrics, cap table, contracts
  • Register on FundingRound.in and Tracxn to get discovered
  • Hire CA + CS for compliance — critical before VC due diligence
  • Apply for FFS-backed VC funds through SIDBI network

🌍 Expand & Export

  • EXIM Bank export credit for product companies going global
  • FIEO — Federation of Indian Export Organisations for market entry
  • Startup India Global Program for entering US/EU markets
  • Setup Singapore holding company for foreign investment ease
  • Apply for DPIIT's global acceleration programs

🏆 IPO / Exit Planning

  • BSE SME or NSE Emerge IPO from ₹25 Cr market cap
  • SEBI's reduced IPO compliance for startups (DPIIT recognised)
  • M&A: Register intent on Tata, Reliance, Infosys accelerators
  • ESOPs strategy to retain founding team through exits
  • Secondary buyout via PE firms (Warburg Pincus, KKR India)
